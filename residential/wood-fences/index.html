What Type of Wood Is Used for Fences?

When it comes to choosing a fence, one of the most important decisions is what type of wood to use. There are a variety of options available, and each has its own advantages and disadvantages.

  • One popular choice is cedar. Cedar is durable and resists rot well, making it a good option for wet or humid climates. It is also relatively light, making it easier to work with during installation.
  • Another common choice is pressure-treated pine. Pine is an inexpensive option that is easy to find. However, it is not as durable as other types of wood and may require more frequent repairs or replacement.

When choosing a fence, it is important to consider the climate and the level of maintenance you are willing to perform. By taking these factors into account, you can select the best type of wood for your needs.
