Chain Link Fence Styles, Costs, and Uses in New Orleans


Chain link is the most affordable fencing option in New Orleans, which is why you see it around homes, businesses, schools, and ball fields all over the metro. Cost comes down to three things: the height, the wire gauge, and whether you add a vinyl coating or privacy slats. This guide covers the common heights, what each setup runs, and when chain link makes more sense than wood or aluminum. When you want a quote, TurnKey Fences installs residential chain link and commercial chain link fencing across the area.

The basic parts of a chain link fence include the mesh, posts, rails, and hardware. Each part plays a role in the fence’s strength and durability.

There are different gauges and coatings for chain link fences. The right choice depends on the need for strength or weather resistance.

  • Mesh is the fabric of the fence.
  • Posts support the structure.
  • Rails connect posts at the top.
  • Hardware holds everything together.
  • Coatings can be vinyl or zinc-based to protect against Rust.

Provider Introduction

  • In New Orleans’ humid climate, chain link fences must withstand moisture. Severe weather like hurricanes also tests their resilience.
  • These fences outline properties clearly. This is important during Mardi Gras when crowds fill the streets.
  • For homeowners, affordability makes chain link popular too. It offers effective security without high costs.

Chain Link Fence Applications in New Orleans

Residential Properties

For residential properties, fences are used properties to secure and set boundaries from outsiders. 

Home Security

  • Chain link fences are a popular choice for homeowners in New Orleans. They can be adjusted to various heights, enhancing home security and privacy. Mesh sizes and patterns vary, fitting different needs. For instance, smaller mesh can deter intruders better than larger patterns.
  • Homeowners often opt for personalized gate designs. These gates add character while maintaining security at entry points.

Privacy

In residential areas, chain link fencing is part of effective security strategies. It works well with surveillance systems without obstructing camera views. The ease of installation is a key advantage for homeowners looking to secure their property quickly without sacrificing perimeter defense.

Commercial Properties

For commercial areas, fences are often used as security and as part of complying with the city’s regulations. 

Business Security

  • Businesses in New Orleans use chain link fences with slats for extra privacy. This option provides seclusion without the need for solid walls that block all visibility. Chain link fences offer an ideal balance between transparency and privacy, creating private outdoor spaces even in urban settings.
  • Comparatively, they allow more visibility than many other types of fencing, which helps maintain an open feel while still delineating boundaries clearly.

Regulatory Compliance

  • For commercial properties requiring heightened protection, reinforced chain link options are available. These fences integrate seamlessly with access control systems to enhance business premises’ safety measures effectively.
  • Case studies highlight how businesses have experienced fewer break-ins after installing these durable barriers around their perimeters.

Emergency Repairs

Keeping a chain link fence clean can greatly extend its lifespan. Simple upkeep tasks like removing debris and occasional washing can prevent rust and other damage. In the Gulf Coast region, it’s wise to perform seasonal inspections due to the unique climate challenges such as humidity and storms.

Here’s a checklist for regular inspections we do at TurnKey Fences:

  • Look for signs of rust or corrosion.
  • Check for loose or damaged links.
  • Ensure gate hinges and latches function properly.
  • To avoid repairs, keep vegetation trimmed back from your fence line. Plants can warp or bend the fencing material over time if not managed correctly.
