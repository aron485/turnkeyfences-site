What is Aluminum?

Aluminum fencing is a popular choice for residential and commercial properties due to its versatility, durability, and aesthetic appeal. This fencing is made of high-quality aluminum alloy and offers many features and benefits, making it an attractive option for different uses.

One of the key advantages of aluminum fencing is its strength and durability. It is resistant to rust, corrosion, and weather damage, making it suitable for outdoor use in different climates. Additionally, aluminum fencing is lightweight, which makes it easier to install and handle compared to other materials like wrought iron or steel.

The low-maintenance nature of aluminum fencing is another advantage, as it does not require regular painting or staining to maintain its appearance. With a wide variety of styles, colors, and designs available, aluminum fencing can enhance the curb appeal of any property while providing security and privacy.

Whether used for residential or commercial purposes, aluminum fencing is an excellent choice for those seeking a durable and visually appealing fencing solution.

Benefits of Aluminum Fences

Aluminum fences offer numerous advantages that make them a popular choice among homeowners and businesses alike. Some of the key benefits of aluminum fences include:

  1. Durability: Aluminum fences are highly durable and resistant to rust, corrosion, and weather conditions. They can withstand New Orleans’s challenging climate, including high humidity, saltwater exposure, and strong winds.
  2. Low Maintenance: Unlike other fencing materials, aluminum fences require minimal maintenance. They do not need to be painted or stained, and they are easy to clean. This saves you time and effort in the long run.
  3. Versatile Designs: Aluminum fences come in a wide range of styles, colors, and heights, allowing you to choose the perfect design that complements your property. Whether you prefer a traditional or modern look, there’s an aluminum fence style that suits your taste.
  4. Security: Aluminum fences provide a strong and secure barrier around your property, deterring intruders and keeping your loved ones and belongings safe. They can be equipped with additional security features such as gates, locks, and access control systems.
  5. Value Enhancement: Installing an aluminum fence can increase the value of your property. It adds curb appeal and creates a visually appealing boundary that potential buyers or tenants will appreciate.

Choosing the Right Aluminum Fence Contractor

Selecting the best aluminum fence contractor is crucial to ensure a successful and hassle-free installation process. Consider the following factors when making your decision:

Frequently Asked Questions

Fencing Questions, Answered

Is an aluminum fence a good choice for the New Orleans climate?
Aluminum is one of the best options here because it will not rust the way iron or steel can, which matters a lot with our humidity and salt air. The powder-coated finish stands up to rain and sun without flaking for many years. It is a smart pick for homes closer to the lake or coast where moisture is relentless.
How long does an aluminum fence last?
A quality powder-coated aluminum fence generally lasts 20 to 30 years or more, even in our wet climate, because the metal does not corrode like steel. The finish may fade slightly over decades of sun exposure, but the structure stays sound. It is one of the lowest-worry fences you can install down here.
What does aluminum fencing typically cost?
Installed aluminum fencing usually runs somewhere around $25 to $45 per linear foot, depending on the height, grade, and style you choose. It costs more than chain link but less than true wrought iron, while giving you a similar ornamental look. We will measure your property and give you a free, no-obligation estimate.
How much maintenance does aluminum fencing need?
Very little, which is one of its biggest selling points. An occasional rinse with a hose to clear off pollen, dirt, or salt residue is usually all it needs. There is no sanding, painting, or rust treatment like you would have with iron.
What are aluminum fences commonly used for?
They are popular for front yards, pool enclosures, and decorative perimeters where you want an open, elegant look without blocking the view. The design meets most pool-code safety requirements, which makes it a favorite for backyard pools around the metro. It also works well as a lighter, lower-maintenance stand-in for ornamental iron.
