Pros and Cons of a Chain Link Fence for Your Business

If you’re considering installing a fence for your business, you may be wondering if a chain link fence is the right choice. Here are some pros and cons of commercial chain link fences to help you decide.

Pros:

  • A chain-link fence is a very durable option that can withstand a lot of wear and tear.
  • It is also a very affordable fencing option for businesses.
  • They can withstand high winds and heavy rain, making them ideal for coastal areas like New Orleans.
  • Chain link fences are not only easy to install and maintain, but they now come with a vinyl coating to prevent rusting and corrosion. Vinyl-coated chain link fences are also available in different colors to match your business’s color scheme. This added layer also creates a more attractive look.
  • They can also be easily customized with add-ons such as barbed wire or privacy slats.

Cons:

  • Chain-link fences can rust over time.
  • They may also sag or become loose if not properly maintained.
  • Some businesses may not like the industrial look of chain link fences.

Overall, commercial chain link fences have a lot of benefits, but there are a few drawbacks to consider as well. Weigh the pros and cons carefully to decide if a chain link fence is right for your business.

Chain Link Fence Quality

It’s important to note that NOT all chain link fences are created equal. When planning to have a chain-link fence installed on your commercial property, be sure to ask the contractor about the quality of the fence.

Commercial-grade chain link fences are made with thicker, stronger wire and have a better overall finish.

This type of fence will be more durable and last longer than a residential-grade chain-link fence. It’s worth the investment to get a higher-quality fence for your business.

Also, make sure that the contractor uses galvanized posts to further extend the life of your commercial chain link fence.

How Much Does a Commercial Chain-Link Fence Cost?

Chain-link fences that are 4 to 12 feet tall, on average, cost $12 to $35 per linear foot installed. Just the materials for these same types of fences cost $7 to $19 per linear foot on average.

Also, commercial chain-link fencing is stronger than other kinds, lasts longer, and has a thicker 9-to-12-gauge wire.

Why Choose Chain Link Fence for Your Commercial Property

Chain-link fencing is an ideal solution for commercial and industrial applications because of its strength, durability, and low maintenance requirements. It can withstand high winds, heavy rain, and other harsh weather conditions.

Chain-link fences are also highly visible, which makes them an excellent choice for security applications. They can deter potential intruders and help authorities spot unauthorized activity. And, because they’re easy to install and remove, they can be used as a temporary security measure during construction projects or special events.
